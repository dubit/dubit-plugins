# dubit plugins

The **{{displayName}}** plugin brings dubit.ask into Claude: ask research questions about children, teens
and families and get answers, with charts, from dubit's own data. You need {{eligibility}}; to ask for
access, contact dubit at https://dubit.io/contact.

- **Claude Desktop:** open **Customize > Plugins > Add > Add marketplace**, enter `{{repository}}`, add
  **{{displayName}}**, then choose **Connect** on the plugin's **Connectors** tab and sign in with your
  dubit account. Full steps, including Team and Enterprise plans, and what data it sends:
  [plugins/{{pluginName}}/README.md](plugins/{{pluginName}}/README.md).
- **ChatGPT:** coming soon.

**Added this marketplace for the earlier pilot?** Choose **Check for updates** on it in
**Customize > Plugins**, then add **{{displayName}}**. The pilot plugins {{retiredPlugins}} no longer
connect; remove them.
